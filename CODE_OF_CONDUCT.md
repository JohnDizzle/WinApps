# Community code of conduct

Everyone participating in WinApps issues, pull requests, and discussions should treat others with respect.

- Give constructive feedback about the work.
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Respect privacy and boundaries.
- Avoid harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information.

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation when needed. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through an available contact method on [JohnDizzle's GitHub profile](https://github.com/JohnDizzle). Do not publish private details in an issue. This policy applies to project spaces and to conduct representing the project.
