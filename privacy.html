Privacy policy · Updated October 18, 2026

Your data stays on your PC.

WinApps does not collect or send your personal information, installed-app inventory, file contents, or application settings to us. It contains no external analytics services, advertising trackers, telemetry uploads, or automatic crash-report uploads.

What WinApps accesses locally

WinApps reads information about installed applications, accessible package manifests, application icons, local files, and supported Windows ApplicationData settings to show them to you. App activity features also read window titles, process identities and executable locations, RAM and CPU measurements, process and thread counts, and Windows startup registrations and shortcuts. This information is processed on your computer; activity measurements are held in memory.

WinApps can change an existing supported setting when you click Save, remove a selected package after you confirm uninstall, and terminate matching running processes after confirmation. Switching to an app brings its window forward. Opening Windows startup settings hands control to Windows Settings.

Local storage

WinApps caches the application library in %LOCALAPPDATA%\WinApps\library.json and application icons under %LOCALAPPDATA%\WinApps\IconCache. Window size, position, and maximized state are saved in %LOCALAPPDATA%\WinApps\window.json so your window placement can be restored. Compact-view position, size, and the remember-placement preference are saved separately in %LOCALAPPDATA%\WinApps\compact-window.json. Settings lets you turn off remembering compact placement. These local files stay on your computer and are not uploaded to the maintainers. Refresh applications updates the library cache. You can remove these caches while WinApps is closed; it will recreate them when needed.

Policy change: optional app-interaction logging

Updated October 18, 2026. Everyone can choose whether to enable Log app interactions in the title-bar Settings menu. Logging is off by default. Dismissing the policy notice does not enable it, and no purchase or paid membership is required.

When enabled, WinApps records daily counts of the app overviews you open inside WinApps, the app suggestions displayed, and whether you select an app from a displayed suggestion set. These are counts grouped by day and application identity, rather than a timestamped browsing log. It does not record your interactions inside other applications, keystrokes, file contents, or a history of window titles.

Counts are stored locally in %LOCALAPPDATA%\WinApps\app-views.json. The default retention is 30 days; Settings offers 7, 30, or 90 days. Older choices gradually count less, with a seven-day half-life, and repeated app views are capped at 20 per app per day. A hit means a selection from a displayed suggestion set, counted at most once per set. The hit rate describes those selections; it is not a guarantee that a suggestion is correct.

Turning the toggle off stops new interaction logging and personalized ranking. Clear suggestion history removes saved interaction counts and hit metrics while preserving your preferences. Nothing in this history is uploaded to us. The dismissed policy-notice revision is stored separately in %LOCALAPPDATA%\WinApps\suggestion-policy-notice.txt.

External links and applications

Microsoft Learn is Microsoft’s documentation service, operated independently of WinApps. Review Microsoft’s privacy statement for Microsoft services.

Opening a file with its default application or launching another app hands control to that application. Its data practices are governed by its own policy. Windows and Microsoft Store also have their own data practices.

If the pages are later hosted online, the hosting provider may process normal web requests under its own policy. External links connect only when you follow them.
